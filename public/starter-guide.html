Five small steps

That page is your checklist in the app, and each step there shows a check mark when it is done. Open Set up Suite
1

Connect your AI

To do

Your assistant, your daily briefings and your insights run on your own AI key. Connect it first and they are ready the moment you finish. About five minutes.

You will know it worked whenYou see Key configured in green.

This must be an OpenAI API key, which is different from a ChatGPT account, so a ChatGPT login will not work here. We suggest creating a separate key just for the Command Suite, so you can see exactly what it costs. Setting up should cost you less than $5, one time, and day-to-day use is minimal, about $0.20 a day. Those are estimates, not guarantees: your own use sets the real cost, and OpenAI bills your own account directly. LifeCharter does not receive any of it and cannot control it. The Travel Partner in the corner of the screen does not use your key, so asking it for help costs you nothing, and you do not need to upgrade your ChatGPT plan. If this step feels fiddly, bring it to Command Suite Office Hours and we will do it together.

Open AI settingsGet an OpenAI key
Optional, and a big time-saver: teach your own AI helper

If you already use ChatGPT or Claude, let it help you set up the Suite. It already knows you.

  1. In ChatGPT or Claude, create a project called Command Suite, with a folder inside it called Admin.
  2. Open a new chat in that folder and say: We are setting up the LifeCharter Command Suite. Save this to memory.
  3. Back in the Suite, under Settings, then AI Assistant, you will see two questions: How should [your assistant] reply? and What should [your assistant] know about you and how you work? Paste each one into your chat (with a screenshot of the page if you like) and read what comes back.
  4. Edit the answers until they sound like you, then paste them into the Suite. Mostly right is fine. You can change them any time.
  5. For the assessments, make a folder called Brain assessment in the same project. Every section has a Fill this section with my AI button: it copies a ready-made prompt for your ChatGPT or Claude. Paste the prompt in, answer its few questions, then paste its reply back into the Suite and review each answer before you keep it. You can also copy a single question over by hand.

The bigger picture

How it all fits together

What you set up on Day 1 is the big picture: who you are, where your business stands and where you are headed. The everyday work that moves it forward lives right alongside it, in the same place. What you do today moves the score you saw earlier. You do not need any of this in your first week. Here is where each part lives when you are ready.

Sales

  • Pipeline in Clients & Sales shows every open opportunity by stage.
  • Offers & Packages in Growth holds what you sell and its prices.

Marketing

  • Content Calendar in Daily Operations plans and schedules your posts.
  • Campaigns & Broadcasts in Clients & Sales sends your emails.
  • Marketing Plan in Growth holds the thinking behind it.

Caring for your clients

  • Contacts keeps everything about one person on one screen, with a full timeline of every touch, so no client slips through the cracks.
  • Calendars gives you booking pages.
  • Testimonials helps you gather kind words.

And all of it is coached. On the Monday Alignment Anchor, we look at the same picture you do.

Questions

You might be wondering

Do I have to finish everything this week?

No. The checklist is a path, not a deadline. Go at the pace that feels good.

Is my information private?

Yes. Each account sees only its own information, and your AI uses only what is in your own account.

What if I'm not a technology person?

You do not have to be. Every step says where to click, and we do the harder parts together in Command Suite Office Hours.

Can I bring my team in?

Yes. You invite them in Settings and choose what each person can see and do.

Do I need my own email service to email my contacts?

Yes, a free Resend account of your own. It delivers your campaigns, broadcasts and booking emails from your own domain, so the sending is yours and counts on your own plan. In Contacts, open Email sending and follow the three steps. Nothing is emailed to your contacts until it is set up.

What if I skip a step?

Nothing breaks. Your assistant simply knows a little less about you until you come back to it.
