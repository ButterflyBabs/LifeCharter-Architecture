## 1. Who this is between

This agreement is between **[Business name]** ("we", "us", the account owner) and **[Affiliate name]** ("you", the affiliate).

It starts on the date you accept it and stays in place until either of us ends it (section 12).

## 2. What an affiliate does

You tell people about our products and services using a tracked link we give you. When a person you sent becomes a customer, we pay you a commission on what they pay, under the rules below.

You are an independent partner, not our employee, agent, or legal representative. You decide how and when you share your link. You cannot sign anything or make promises on our behalf.

## 3. Your link and how long it remembers

- We give you one tracked link for each product. Each link has its own code (for example `lccommandsuite.com/r/your-code`).
- When someone clicks your link, we count the click and remember them for **365 days** on that device and browser. If they sign up on one of our forms, book a call, or buy within those 365 days, the sign-up, booking, or purchase is credited to you.
- **Each link lasts 365 days** from the day it was made or last renewed. We send you and the account owner a reminder email about 30 days before it ends, and it can be renewed for another 365 days. A link that has ended stops crediting new sign-ups. People who were already credited to you keep their credit.
- **First affiliate wins.** The first affiliate a person comes through is the one who gets credit for that person. Clicking a different affiliate's link later does not move them.
- If a person's browser blocks or clears the memory (some browsers and privacy settings do this), we may not be able to credit them to you. We do not guarantee that every referral will be tracked.
- We can also credit a person to you by hand if we agree they came from you.

## 4. What you may and may not say

You may:

- Share your link on your own website, social posts, emails to people who have agreed to hear from you, podcasts, videos, and conversations.
- Describe our products in your own words, using only what we have published about them.
- Share your honest experience.

You must:

- **Say clearly that you are an affiliate** and may be paid if someone buys through your link. Put it where people will see it, in plain words (for example "I'm an affiliate and may earn a commission"). The rules on this come from advertising law and from each platform, and you are responsible for following them.
- Be truthful. Only say things you know to be true.
- Follow the rules of the platform you are posting on, and the rules about email and text marketing in your country and state, including getting permission before you message people.

You must not:

- Promise or imply **results, income, or outcomes** that we have not published, or say a customer "will" earn, save, or achieve anything. Share your own experience as your own experience.
- Make **medical, legal, tax, or financial claims** about our products.
- Say or suggest that you are us, that you work for us, or that we endorse something we have not agreed to.
- Offer refunds, guarantees, discounts, bonuses, or payment terms that we have not published. Quote prices only as we publish them.
- Send spam, buy lists, scrape contact details, or add people to a list without their permission.
- Use misleading, deceptive, or hidden links, pop-ups, or redirects, or click your own link to earn commission on your own purchase [attorney: decide whether self-purchases are allowed].
- Use our business name, our brand names, or our logos in a way that makes your page look like ours. [Attorney/owner: decide whether paid ads that bid on our brand names are allowed. Suggested: not allowed without written permission.]
- Use our content, names, or images beyond what we have given you to use for promotion.

If you break these rules we may pause or remove your link, hold or cancel unpaid commissions that came from the broken rule, and end this agreement.

## 5. What earns a commission

A sale earns you a commission when all of these are true:

1. The person was credited to you under section 3.
2. They paid us money for a product or service we offer, and the payment was actually collected.
3. The link they came through earns on that kind of payment (below).
4. The payment has not been refunded (section 8).

**Each link has one of two settings, and we tell you which one yours has:**

- **Every payment.** You earn on every payment the credited person makes for the products your link covers. This includes later payments on a subscription or payment plan. Each payment is credited once.
- **Implementation fee only.** You earn only on the one-time implementation fee. You do **not** earn on monthly or other later payments, and you do not earn on other products the person buys.

If a payment includes both an implementation fee and something else, a link set to "implementation fee only" earns only on the implementation fee part, after any discount.

## 6. How the commission is worked out

- **The commission is a percentage** of the amount the customer paid toward the sale (after any discount we gave them). [Attorney: decide whether sales tax or shipping are excluded. Today the system works from the amount paid.]
- **Which percentage applies**, in this order: (1) the percentage we agreed with you for that specific product, (2) the percentage we set on that product, (3) the percentage on your link, (4) your default percentage. If none of these is set yet, the sale is held for review until we set one. We will tell you the percentage in writing or on your dashboard.
- The percentage is fixed on each sale when it is credited. If we change your percentage later, the change applies to sales credited after the change, unless we agree otherwise in writing.
- Commission is calculated in US dollars. [Attorney: confirm currency wording.]
- We show you each sale, its commission, its status, and the date it can be paid on your private dashboard and in a monthly report you can download.

## 7. The payout hold

To leave room for refunds, each commission is held for a number of days after the customer's payment. **Your hold is [30] days** unless we tell you otherwise in writing.

- The "payable date" for a sale is the sale date plus the hold. Your dashboard shows it. A commission is "ready to pay" from that date.
- A change to your hold applies to sales credited after the change.
- Nothing is paid before its payable date.

## 8. Refunds

- **A full refund inside the hold.** If the customer is fully refunded before the payable date and we have not paid the commission, the sale is marked **void**. No commission is due on it. The sale stays in your records with the reason and the Stripe refund reference, so you can see what happened.
- **A partial refund inside the hold.** We review it. The commission is not changed automatically; we decide whether to reduce or void it and tell you.
- **A refund after the hold has ended, or when there was no hold, and the commission has not been paid.** We review it and decide whether any commission is due.
- **A refund after we have already paid you.** We do not take anything back automatically. We flag it and review it. We may [attorney: choose one: deduct it from your future commissions / ask you to repay it / forgive it], and we will tell you before we do.
- Refunds are processed by our payment provider. A refund that we do not learn about right away is handled when we do. Handling the same refund twice never changes a sale twice.
- We are not able to detect chargebacks or payment disputes automatically yet. [Attorney: add wording that a lost dispute is treated like a refund.]

## 9. How and when you are paid

- We pay by [bank transfer / PayPal / other] to the account you give us. You are responsible for giving us correct payment details.
- Commissions that have passed their payable date are paid [monthly, by the 15th of the following month / other schedule]. We may pay sooner.
- We may set a minimum payout of [$__]. Smaller amounts roll over to the next payout.
- Payment is made in US dollars. Transfer fees charged by your bank or payment service are yours.
- When we pay, we mark the commission as paid and record it in our books. If a commission is marked paid by mistake, tell us and we will correct it.

## 11. No guarantee of earnings

**This is not a guarantee of income.** We make no promise that you will earn any commission, any particular amount, or anything at all. Results depend on you, on the people you reach, and on whether they choose to buy. Examples we share are examples, not predictions. We may change, pause, or stop our products and offers at any time. If a product is withdrawn, commissions end for that product from that date; commissions already earned follow sections 7 to 9.

## 12. Ending this agreement, and pausing

- **You may leave at any time** by telling us in writing. Please stop using your links.
- **We may pause or end** your account or any link at any time, with or without a reason, by telling you. We will tell you promptly if we do it because of a broken rule in section 4.
- A paused or ended link stops crediting **new** sign-ups. People who were already credited to you keep their credit.
- **Commissions already earned** and not voided by a refund stay payable under sections 7 to 9 after the agreement ends. We may hold or cancel commissions that came from a breach of section 4.
- Sections 4 (what you may not say, as to anything already published), 8, 10, 11, 13, and 14 continue after the agreement ends.

## 13. Privacy and information

- **What we collect through your link:** that a click happened (with the time and the site it came from), a small cookie in the visitor's browser that records which affiliate sent them, and, when the person signs up, books, or buys, the details they give us, which we keep as our own customer and contact records.
- **What you see:** your clicks, the first names of people credited to you, and your sales, commissions, and payments. We do not show you contact details of the people you referred, their email addresses, or what else they do with us.
- **What you may do with it:** keep your dashboard and reports private to you, use them only to manage your own affiliate activity, and do not share or sell them.
- **People you send to us:** only send people to us who you have a lawful basis to contact, and do not give us anyone's personal information unless you have the right to.

## 15. Questions for AmiLynne and the attorney

1. **Hold length.** Every new client account is set up with a 30-day hold. Is 30 days the standard for the program?
2. **Partial refunds.** The system flags a partial refund for review instead of reducing the commission automatically. Should a partial refund reduce the commission in proportion, automatically?
3. **Refund after payout.** What happens when a refund comes after you have paid? (Section 8 leaves this open on purpose.)
4. **Chargebacks and disputes.** These are not detected automatically today. Do we want them treated like refunds, and do we want to build detection?
5. **Self-purchase.** May an affiliate earn commission on their own purchase?
6. **Brand-name ads.** Can affiliates run paid ads on our brand names? Suggested: no.
7. **Sales tax in the commission base.** Today the commission is worked out from the amount paid. Should tax be taken out first?
8. **Payout schedule and minimum.** Monthly on the 15th? A minimum payout? (Section 9 is blank on purpose. The Suite records "paid" but does not send money.)
9. **Clients' own agreements.** Every client account has affiliates. Should this be a template each client can put their own business name on, or only for LifeCharter?
10. **Acceptance.** How do affiliates accept it (a checkbox on their dashboard, a signed copy, an emailed "I agree")? The system has an "agreement on" date field for each affiliate but does not collect acceptance itself.
